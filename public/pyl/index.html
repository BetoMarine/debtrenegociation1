The work

Three live products. Same weight. Each app uses Plan Your Life purple and teal on a calm light canvas — not this dark marketing page.

Live Fix Hong Kong · banked borrowers

Right Door · 正確的門

A free PWA for banked Hong Kong borrowers under stress. Write to hardship / debt programmes the bank must offer but won’t advertise — not the branch account manager. You prepare the letter on your phone. You send it.

  • On this phone only. Pack, photos, HKID, and amounts stay here unless you share them.
  • No account. We do not upload, store, or collect your data. Uninstall wipes it.
  • Published hardship programmes — not the branch account manager. We never contact a bank, lender, or anyone else in your name.

What it is not

Not a debt mill. Not a collector. Not someone who calls the bank for you. Not a hidden department. Restructuring is usually reported to credit agencies — the point is avoiding a 60-day default, bankruptcy, or write-off, not a clean score.

Live Fix · Stabilize Hong Kong · foreign domestic workers

Sunday Pack

A free PWA for foreign domestic workers and helpers in Hong Kong. Build a floor, stop new loans, and walk to the Enrich door with a 1-page briefing you prepare on this phone. Not a literacy lecture on its own.

  • On this phone only. Separate store from Right Door. Uninstall wipes it.
  • No account. We do not talk to Enrich or any lender for you. Deep links only when you tap.
  • Honest footer: not affiliated with Enrich. Not a credit report. Not legal advice.

What it is not

Not a remittance rail. Not a wallet. Not someone who books Enrich or messages a lender for you. Crisis doors are tap-to-call / WhatsApp only. Own Safari home-screen icon — not behind a chooser with Right Door.

Live New Me Hong Kong · timeline + %

Fortune Teller

A free PWA for New Me: a today→future timeline with a chance-% on each pin. The path is Fix → Stabilize → Plan → Invest. Rebuild first. If debt is on fire, it routes you to Right Door or Sunday Pack. Not a retirement gadget. Not Envizage.

  • On this phone only. Separate store from Right Door and Sunday Pack. Uninstall wipes it.
  • Illustrative model. Not regulated advice. Not a product sale. Not affiliated with HSBC.
  • Execute elsewhere with a licensed intermediary. Own Safari home-screen icon — not behind a chooser.

What it is not

Not years-to-retirement as the hero. Not a fund we sell. Not live market marks. Not a green “you're set” score. Absurd inputs (no income, no savings, a HK$15M house) hard-fail. The URL is stable: /fortune/.

Research

Earlier work

PYL Invest

Earlier work: a planning sandbox that tied savings and investment choices to life events, so people could see trade-offs before they committed money. It is not the current flagship and is not offered as advice or a live brokerage.

How we work

You act. We point to published doors. We label what is live.

You act

We build tools you run yourself. If a letter has to go to a bank, you send it in your name. We never become the middleman.

Published doors

When money or hardship is involved, we point to hardship programmes the bank already publishes — not the branch account manager. No invented units. No “we know someone.”

Honest labels

Live, research, or earlier work. If it is not shipped, we say so. That is why Offshore is research and Right Door, Sunday Pack, and Fortune Teller are the products we ask you to open.
