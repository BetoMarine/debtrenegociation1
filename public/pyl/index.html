The work

One product is live. The rest is labeled honestly.

Live Featured · Hong Kong

Right Door · 正確的門

A free PWA for banked Hong Kong borrowers who lost hours or a job and need to write to the published hardship door — IDRP or the bank’s own workout unit — not the branch account manager. You prepare the letter on your phone. You send it.

  • On this phone only. Pack, photos, HKID, and amounts stay here unless you share them.
  • No account. We do not upload, store, or collect your data. Uninstall wipes it.
  • Published doors only. We never contact a bank, lender, or anyone else in your name.

What it is not

Not a debt mill. Not a collector. Not someone who calls the bank for you. Not a hidden department. Restructuring is usually reported to credit agencies — the point is avoiding a 60-day default, bankruptcy, or write-off, not a clean score.

Research

Offshore

Research into tools for migrant domestic workers in Hong Kong — people who send money home and are poorly served by banks. This is not a shipped remittance product. There is no live payment rail, no wallet, and no claim of a working remittance ecosystem. We are still learning the job before we build.

Earlier work

PYL Invest

Earlier work: a planning sandbox that tied savings and investment choices to life events, so people could see trade-offs before they committed money. It is not the current flagship and is not offered as advice or a live brokerage.

How we work

A small studio. Ship the useful thing. Say what is not ready.

You stay in control

We build tools you run yourself. If a letter has to go to a bank, you send it in your name. We never become the middleman.

Published paths only

When money or hardship is involved, we point to doors the bank or regulator already publishes. No invented units. No “we know someone.”

Label the stage

Live, research, or earlier work. If it is not shipped, we say so. That is why Offshore is research and Right Door is the product we ask you to open.
